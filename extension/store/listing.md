## Description

Save the article now. Read it properly later.

Hold Shelf for Chrome sends pages and links to your Hold Shelf library, so a
good read can wait until you have time for it.

SAVE FROM CHROME

- Click the toolbar button to save the current page.
- Right-click a page or link and choose Save to Hold Shelf.
- Get a quick confirmation without leaving the page.
- Already saved it? The extension tells you without creating a duplicate.

COME BACK TO YOUR LIBRARY

Open hold-shelf.com to read, search, and organize what you saved. Hold Shelf
keeps a readable copy of supported articles and remembers your place in the
reader. Use tags and favorites to organize your library, and choose a light or
dark theme.

GET STARTED

Sign in at hold-shelf.com with GitHub, then pin the extension to your toolbar.
If you need to sign in while saving, the extension opens Hold Shelf so you can
finish there. A Hold Shelf account is required.

PERMISSIONS AND PRIVACY

The extension sends only the page or link URL you explicitly choose to save.
It does not monitor your browsing history or save pages in the background.

- activeTab: read the current page URL when you click the toolbar button.
- contextMenus: add Save page and Save link to the right-click menu.
- scripting: show save confirmation or an error on the page.
- hold-shelf.com access: save to your library using your signed-in session.
